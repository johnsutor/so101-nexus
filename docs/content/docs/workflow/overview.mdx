---
title: Record, clone, reinforce
description: Take a simulated SO-101 from demonstrations to a trained policy.
---

One workflow connects demonstrations to a trained policy:

1. **Record.** Control a simulated follower with an SO-100 or SO-101 leader arm. Save a LeRobot dataset.
2. **Clone.** Train an actor to reproduce the demonstrations with behavior cloning.
3. **Reinforce.** Fine-tune with PPO while a behavior-cloning loss anchors the actor to the demonstrations.

Follow [Teleoperation](/docs/workflow/teleoperation) to record your dataset, then
[Training](/docs/workflow/training#quick-start) to train and evaluate a policy checkpoint.
The training script runs the clone and reinforce stages together.

## Start without hardware

Use the published demonstrations in the [training quick start](/docs/workflow/training#quick-start).
For a hosted GPU, open the
[BC + PPO Colab notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/github/johnsutor/so101-nexus/blob/main/examples/bc_ppo_warp_colab.ipynb).
Select a GPU runtime and run all cells.

To use your own recordings later, set `--demo-repo` to your dataset in the same training script.

## Transfer between backends

Record with MuJoCo and train in parallel with Warp.
Training derives delta-action labels from consecutive recorded joint positions.
See [Control modes](/docs/concepts/control-modes) for the action contract.

The backends share physics defaults, but contacts and trajectories can differ.
Evaluate the policy on its target backend. See [Backends](/docs/concepts/backends#physics-divergence) for the differences.
