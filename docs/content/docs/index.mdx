SO101-Nexus takes an SO-101 robot from demonstrations to a trained policy in one installable
library. It connects physical leader-arm teleoperation, LeRobot-compatible dataset recording,
Gymnasium manipulation environments, and training baselines, all on MuJoCo.

The pipeline is deliberately boring: **record** demonstrations, **clone** them with behavior
cloning, then **reinforce** with RL.

## What you get

- **[Teleoperation](/docs/workflow/teleoperation).** Drive a simulated follower with a
  physical SO-100 or SO-101 leader arm and save LeRobot v3 datasets, cameras included.
- **[Six manipulation tasks](/docs/environments).** PickLift, PickAndPlace, StackCube, Touch,
  LookAt, and Move, all on the standard Gymnasium API.
- **[Imitation learning and RL](/docs/workflow/training).** Behavior cloning from your demos,
  then PPO fine-tuning that keeps anchoring to them.
- **[Configurable scenes](/docs/concepts/customization).** Swap objects, add distractors,
  randomize colors and spawn regions, tune rewards, and choose observation components.
- **[LeRobot-native](/docs/concepts/lerobot).** Datasets, processors, and hardware drivers
  come from LeRobot rather than a parallel implementation.
- **[GPU-parallel Warp backend](/docs/concepts/backends).** The same six tasks as batched
  vector environments for large-scale RL (optional, experimental, NVIDIA only).
